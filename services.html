The Ecosystem Structure

Moving beyond disconnected activities, we build a permanent, structured environment inside the school. We integrate real-world problem solving directly into the student experience.

The IRDME Journey

Students don't just "make projects." They are guided through a rigorous cycle of Innovation, Research, Development, Management, and Entrepreneurship.

Through industrial visits, mentorship, and showcases, students graduate with genuine professional exposure and readiness, not just a certificate.
