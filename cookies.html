1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Scalora Schools ("Scalora Schools", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website: https://scaloraschools.com/

Our website is intended to provide information about our educational programs, initiatives, partnerships and services. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how cookies may be used on our website.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember certain information and provide a better browsing experience. Cookies may be stored temporarily during your visit or remain on your device for a specified period.

3. How We Use Cookies

Scalora Schools may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Maintaining essential website functionality
  • Improving website security
  • Remembering basic website preferences
  • Understanding how the website is being used, where applicable
  • Improving the overall user experience

We do not intentionally use cookies to collect sensitive personal information.

4. Types of Cookies

4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies may be required for the website to operate correctly and securely. They may support basic functions such as:

  • Website security
  • Session management
  • Basic functionality
  • Technical operation of the website

These cookies generally cannot be disabled through a cookie preference tool because the website may not function properly without them.

4.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may be used to remember certain preferences or settings selected by visitors. These cookies are used only where the relevant functionality is implemented on the website.

4.3 Analytics Cookies

At present, Scalora Schools does not intentionally use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Vercel Analytics or Vercel Speed Insights on the website. If analytics services are introduced in the future, this Cookie Policy may be updated accordingly.

4.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

Scalora Schools does not intentionally use advertising cookies for targeted advertising on the current website. If marketing, advertising or tracking technologies such as Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag or similar services are introduced, this policy will be updated and users will be provided with appropriate choices where required.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party technologies or services may potentially place cookies or use similar technologies when integrated into our website. We will review third-party services used on the website and update this Cookie Policy when such services are introduced.

6. Managing Cookies

Depending on the functionality available on the website, you may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Allow cookies from selected websites
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some websites.

7. Children's Use of the Website

Our website may be accessed by students, including individuals under the age of 18. We do not intentionally use cookies to collect sensitive personal information from children. Where personal information is collected through future services or programs involving students, appropriate privacy and consent measures will be considered based on the nature of the service and applicable laws.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, technologies, services or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
