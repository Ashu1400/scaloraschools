02 / THE PROBLEM

Education teaches students what to know.
The real world asks what they can do with it.

Today's academic system gives students a strong foundation in subjects and concepts. But real-world problem solving doesn't stop at knowing the answer.

Students need opportunities to explore, research, build, manage, communicate and create — connecting what they learn in the classroom with problems beyond it.

The missing layer isn't another subject. It's an ecosystem.

03 / THE MARKET GAP

THE INDUSTRY FACES A
MASSIVE SKILL SHORTAGE.

Every year, millions of students graduate with theoretical knowledge, but industries report a widening gap in practical, deployable skills. The problem isn't intelligence; it's exposure.

Companies spend months retraining fresh graduates on basics like problem-solving, project management, and cross-functional collaboration. The affected factor isn't just time—it's global innovation.
