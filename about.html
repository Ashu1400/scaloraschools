We're building the ecosystem

02 / WHAT WE FOUND

We found that entrepreneurship
doesn't start with a business.

It starts much earlier.

Students need to learn how to identify problems, think differently, research possibilities, build solutions, manage their work and only then take those solutions into the real world. That discovery became the foundation of IRDME.

03 / INSIGHTS

What we’ve learned from building with students.

01

EXPERIENCE MATTERS

Students understand concepts differently when they have the opportunity to apply them to real problems.

02

ENTREPRENEURSHIP COMES LATER

Before students can build ventures, they need to learn how to identify problems, innovate, research, develop and manage.

03

ECOSYSTEMS BEAT ONE-OFF ACTIVITIES

A single workshop can create excitement. A structured, continuous environment can create sustained student engagement and development.

04

STUDENTS NEED OWNERSHIP

When students move from simply participating to taking responsibility, leadership and accountability become part of the learning experience.

04 / FOUNDERS' MESSAGE

"We built Scalora because we saw a gap between what students learn and what the real world demands."

Our journey started with a simple observation: schools provide excellent academic foundations, but students lack the environment to apply that knowledge to solve real problems.

Pushpendra Singh Ratmani Co-Founder, Scalora Schools

"We envisioned an ecosystem where innovation, research, and entrepreneurship are a structured process."

Scalora Schools is that ecosystem—a place where curiosity turns into competence, and students evolve into leaders before they even graduate. We believe in building the future today.

B S Ashwath Babu Co-Founder, Scalora Schools
